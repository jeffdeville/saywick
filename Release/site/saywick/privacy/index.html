Privacy policy

Last updated September 11, 2026.

Audio and text

Saywick transcribes recordings and applies custom-word replacements on your device. If you select OpenAI cleanup, the transcript and cleanup instructions are sent to OpenAI using your API key. Regular Polish may include relevant custom words. Selecting a saved template sends its prompt for that recording. Other cleanup options run on your device. Audio is not sent to OpenAI. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and requires no user account.

Model download

The first speech-model download connects to Hugging Face and its delivery providers. Those services receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address, to deliver the files. Recording and transcript content is not part of that download request. External links open their respective websites, whose privacy practices apply.

Keyboard and microphone

The containing Saywick app records audio with your permission. Full Access permits the keyboard extension to exchange commands and transcript text with the app through local shared storage. Basic typing works without Full Access. Saywick does not upload what you type.

Contacts

If you choose to add names from Contacts, you select names in the system picker and review them before saving. Only the confirmed names are stored as custom words on your device.

History and deletion

Text and optional audio are stored locally. New installations retain audio for 7 days and text for 30 days by default; you can change these settings in History. Pinned entries are exempt from expiry. Expiry is applied when History refreshes or the app starts. Turning audio retention off affects future dictation recordings; previously saved recordings remain until deleted or expired.

You can delete an entry or its audio from History. Recording files are excluded from device backup. Copies exported, copied, or shared to other apps are subject to those apps’ storage and deletion controls.

Diagnostics

Saywick does not operate a remote crash-report upload service. iOS may collect or share system diagnostics according to your Apple privacy settings. Saywick retains up to 100 recent error events per app process and up to five Apple MetricKit diagnostic payloads locally, excluded from backup. Error events contain operation names, error codes, sanitized domains and app versions, without transcript text or audio. Apple payloads may contain device, process and stack information. Export diagnostics in the app lets you review and choose where to share these files; Saywick does not upload them automatically.
