Saywick support

Start dictating

  1. Open Saywick and grant microphone access when prompted.
  2. Download the on-device English speech model. Allow approximately 731 MB for the model plus room for recordings.
  3. Tap Start listening. Tap Stop dictation to finish, then copy your text.

Set up the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, then select Saywick.
  2. Enable Full Access for local dictation. Basic typing works without it.
  3. Start a recording in Saywick, switch to your destination app, select the Saywick keyboard, then tap Stop to finish and insert.

iOS uses its system keyboard in secure fields, and some apps disable third-party keyboards.

Keep the keyboard ready

With Keep keyboard ready enabled, the microphone can remain active for two minutes between dictations. Active recordings are not ended by that idle timeout. Use End microphone session to release it immediately. You can also add Saywick’s Start dictation and Stop recording actions in Shortcuts.

Turn cleanup off

In Saywick, find Pipeline → Post-processing and choose Off — raw transcript. Apple Intelligence cleanup is enabled by default and runs locally after Stop; it can take extra time. You can turn it off at any time. Custom-word replacements are separate and still apply when enabled.

Interrupted recording or missing keyboard

Open Saywick and check History for preserved text or retained audio. Confirm microphone permission, keyboard installation, and Full Access in Settings. Start a new recording after an audio-route interruption. If the keyboard is absent in just one app or a password field, iOS or that app may require the system keyboard.

Manage recordings

History supports playback, retranscription, export, pinning, and deletion. Removing meeting mode does not remove previously saved recordings.

Share diagnostics

Open Privacy → Export diagnostics in Saywick to prepare recent error events and any Apple diagnostic reports available on the device. Review the file before sharing. It does not include Saywick recordings or transcript text; Apple reports can contain device and process details.

Contact support

A support contact for questions and issue reports will be added before the App Store release. I reply as time permits. When reporting an issue, include your iOS version, Saywick version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not include private recordings or transcripts unless you intend to share them.
